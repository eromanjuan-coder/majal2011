About IV - MAJAL

IV - MAJAL is more than just a section name; it represents a family of 4th-year high school students guided by our adviser, Mrs. Roena Calma. What began in the classroom grew into a bond as strong as forged steel. Despite the passing years, our circle remains intact, defined by a promise to always be there for one another. This page is dedicated to our journey, our milestones, and the lifelong friendship of IV-MAJAL
